WETSAPP Privacy Policy

Wetland Monitoring Application by Nature Seychelles

First published: 10 March 2026

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Introduction

WETSAPP (Wetland Evaluation and Tracking Seychelles) ("the App") is a wetland monitoring application developed as part of the Nature Seychelles Wetlands of Hope project, in collaboration with the LEAP project. The App has been developed under the ‘Improving Climate Adaptation and Environmental Governance through Local People and Co-Management in Seychelles’ initiative funded by the European Commission, to support wetland conservation and monitoring. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App, in accordance with the Seychelles Data Protection Act 2023 ("the Act").

The personal information collected through the App is controlled by Nature Seychelles ("we", "us").

2. Information We Collect

The App collects the following types of information to support wetland monitoring activities:

  • Account Information: Your name, username and email address, organisation and user level, and a securely hashed version of your password, used for authentication and identifying data submissions.
  • Observer Name: The observer or group name entered on each monitoring form.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates captured when recording field observations (flora/fauna surveys, health assessments, pollution reports, etc.). Location is only collected when you actively use the App's monitoring features.
  • Photos: Images captured through the App's camera for documenting environmental conditions, pollution incidents, and biodiversity observations.
  • Environmental Monitoring Data: Field observations including species counts, health assessments, water quality measurements, cleanup activity records, biomass measurements, and other ecological data you enter into the App.
  • Device Information: Your device and operating system type, for managing offline data synchronisation and for security purposes.
  • Activity Log: A record of sign-in, sign-out and password reset events, linked to your username, to keep the App secure.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Recording and storing wetland monitoring data for conservation research and management.
  • Generating environmental reports and statistics for the Nature Seychelles Wetlands of Hope project.
  • Synchronising your offline data with our servers when an internet connection is available.
  • Identifying data submissions by field team members.
  • Keeping the App and its data secure.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the basis of our legitimate interests in running the Wetlands of Hope project and conserving Seychelles' wetlands, as provided for in section 15(2)(f) of the Act. We only collect the information needed for this purpose.

5. Data Storage and Security

Monitoring data is stored locally on your device when offline and synchronised to our secure servers (hosted on Supabase) when connectivity is available. Photos older than 30 days are moved to a Google Drive account controlled by Nature Seychelles. All data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS. Access to the data is restricted, according to each user's role, to authorised members of the Nature Seychelles monitoring team.

Supabase and Google store data on our behalf and may hold it on servers located outside Seychelles.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. It is shared only with the Ministry of Environment, Climate, Energy and Natural Resources, which co-owns the monitoring data, and with the service providers named in section 5, which store it on our behalf. Environmental monitoring data may be used in aggregated, anonymised form for scientific publications, conservation reports, and programme evaluations by Nature Seychelles and its partners.

7. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your device's camera and photo library solely for capturing and attaching photos to monitoring reports. Photos are stored locally and uploaded to our servers as part of your field reports. We do not access your personal photos outside of the App. Please avoid photographing people when recording observations.

8. Location Services

The App uses location services ("When In Use") to record GPS coordinates for monitoring sites. Location data is only captured when you actively submit a monitoring report or record a new site. The App does not track your location in the background.

9. Offline Data

The App is designed to work offline. Data entered while offline, including photos, is saved in the App's own storage on your device and queued for upload when an internet connection becomes available. It stays on your device until it has been uploaded. Please protect your device with a screen lock, because anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to view data that hasn't been uploaded yet. You can review pending uploads within the App.

10. Data Retention

Monitoring data is retained for the duration of the Nature Seychelles Wetlands of Hope project, and may be kept afterwards for scientific research and statistical purposes, as permitted by section 16(3) of the Act. Activity log records are kept for 30 days. You may request deletion of your personal account information by contacting us at the email address below. When an account is deleted, the account itself is closed, but the observations submitted through it, including the observer name recorded with them, are kept for research purposes.

11. Your Rights

Under the Act, you can ask to know what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete, and in some cases ask us to delete it or limit how it is used. To make a request, contact us at the email address below.

Wetland observations submitted through the App are owned by Nature Seychelles and the Ministry, and are not available for download from the App.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal information, you can complain to the Information Commission of Seychelles (www.infocom.sc).

12. Young Participants

Young people under 18 take part in wetland monitoring through accounts held by their teacher or group leader, and do not need an account of their own. When observations are entered, a group or class name should be used rather than individual pupils' names.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
